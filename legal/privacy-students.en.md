# Privacy notice

## Who runs this site

This site is run by **[Organisation name]** for the EMIS × Ahuzat Tzahala project. Questions or requests: **[contact email]**.

## What we collect and why

- **Event registration:** your name, email, phone (language night only, otherwise optional), the languages you speak and your notes. We use them to manage the event and to send you a confirmation and reminder.
- **Podcast:** your name, email, optional phone, interests, preferred language and the podcast time you choose, to find you a partner.
- **Digital support volunteering:** your name, school email, optional phone, your age group, and, if you're under 18, a parent or guardian's name and phone and their agreement. We use them to organise the slots and keep everyone safe.
- **Reports:** what you write in "Report a problem", and contact details only if you choose to give them.

We don't ask for ID numbers, addresses or health details.

## Who sees it

- Only the project's admins and leaders see your contact details.
- Other students never see your contact details.
- A resident you help sees only your first name. A podcast partner gets your contact details once you're paired.
- Residents' phone numbers are shown to you only for the slots you volunteer for, after you accept the code of conduct, and only from 48 hours before the slot.

## How long we keep it

- Event registrations: deleted 90 days after the event.
- Digital support bookings and volunteer places: deleted 90 days after the slot.
- Cancelled sign-ups and call-back requests: deleted after 30 days.
- Your student account: deleted 30 days after you were last signed in, unless you have a place in a slot.
- Podcast sign-ups and problem reports: deleted after one year.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time: email **[contact email]** or ask a project leader. Admins can delete everything stored about a person in one step.

## We don't sell your data

We never sell or share your data for advertising or any other purpose.

## Services we use

- Hosting: Vercel (servers in Frankfurt, Germany).
- Database and admin sign-in: Supabase (Frankfurt, Germany).
- Email: Gmail (Google) or Resend, depending on the set-up.
- Events: Google Calendar (only the public event list; no personal data is sent there).

## If something goes wrong

If personal data is lost or exposed, we will tell the people affected and the school as soon as possible, explain what happened and what we are doing about it, and report it to the authorities where the law requires.
